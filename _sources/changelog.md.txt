# Changelog

## v1.0.0

The first released version: a restructured paclet with a redesigned, consistent
API.

**Algebras and bases**

- Algebras canonicalize to `LieAlgebra["A"|"B"|"C"|"D", rank]`, with `SU[n]`,
  `SO[n]`, `Sp[n]` as shorthands.
- `CartanWeyl[g]` and `Chevalley[g]` return an association
  (`"Cartan"`, `"Raising"`, `"Lowering"`) instead of an `{H, E, F}` list; both are
  shorthands for `Generators[g, "CartanWeyl"|"Chevalley"]`.
- New root-system helpers: `Rank`, `LieAlgebraDimension`, `CartanMatrix`,
  `SimpleRoots`, `PositiveRoots`, `FundamentalWeights`.
- `StandardBasis` renamed to `BasisTransform`; the old per-element accessors and
  `StandardChevalley` removed.
- The sp(2n) standard generators (previously not closed under the bracket) were
  corrected.

**Representation engine**

- `Irrep[g, w]` plus the data functions `RepresentationDimension`, `WeightSystem`,
  `CasimirEigenvalue`, `RepresentationMatrices`, and `HighestWeight`.
- Explicit generator matrices via the highest-weight (Shapovalov) construction, in
  an orthonormal basis, reaching every irrep including the so/sp **spinors**.
- Everything computed in exact arithmetic; see [Validation](validation).

**Young-tableau toolkit**

- `Tableau`, `TensorTableau`, `Psi`, and the `Tableau…` / `Tensor…` operations for
  building, combining, normalizing, and orthogonalizing many-body wavefunctions.

For runnable, up-to-date examples see the [tutorials](tutorials/index); for the
full API, the [reference](reference/index).
